## What the set shows

One still per beat of the climb, in scroll order: the entry gate (the first version's "Tap to
explore" over the dawn-grass plate), the Trailhead, the First Ember, Footholds, the Spark Wakes,
and the Summit. The first five were captured on 2026-09-09 from the live first version during the
audit that measured its cold load (see [The 16 MB front door](/notes/the-16-mb-front-door/)); the
sixth was captured on 2026-09-12 from [the climb](/climb/) as it now runs inside this site.

## Why the summit comes from the port

The first version's summit still shows a far mountain vista whose origin has not yet been recorded
in the media inventory, so that frame stays unpublished until it is. The ported climb draws its
distant ridges procedurally, so its summit frame carries only assets with a provenance record.

## What is in frame

The Wanderer and the Phoenix began as Tripo generations and were finished in Blender by the
author; the grass, fog, embers, and summit clouds are Higgsfield atmosphere plates generated from
the author's prompts (the [V1 case study](/projects/khaylub-com-v1/) states both). Each still is
the author's own screenshot, resized to 1440 or 1600 pixels wide and served through the image
pipeline at the size your screen needs.
